## Interpretation

`public_app` is a policy profile layered on top of the raw scanner. It preserves
the raw `Verdict`, but only sets `safe_to_auto_block` when the combined signal
is suitable for a public-facing app to refuse automatically.

The remaining misses in this generated suite are downgraded because the raw
scanner returns `Flag`, not `Block`. That is intentional for this pass: the
policy layer should not silently convert every suspicious public prompt into a
hard block.
