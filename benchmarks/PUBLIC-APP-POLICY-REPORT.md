## Interpretation

`public_app` is a policy profile layered on top of the raw scanner. It preserves
the raw `Verdict`, but only sets `safe_to_auto_block` when the combined signal
is suitable for a public-facing app to refuse automatically.

The policy layer still avoids converting every suspicious public prompt into a
hard block. The 100% attack result here applies to high-confidence direct
exfiltration and boundary-injection scenarios, while benign public-app controls
remain non-blocking.
